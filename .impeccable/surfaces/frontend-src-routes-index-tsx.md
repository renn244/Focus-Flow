# Landing page surface brief

- **Mode:** Persuade
- **Audience and job:** Students and small project teams deciding whether to
  start a shared FocusFlow workspace; they need to understand the practical
  path from project planning to today's work.
- **Action:** Create an account, or log into an existing one.
- **Truth constraints:** No pricing, analytics, testimonials, customer counts,
  AI, SSO, or enterprise claims. The preview is illustrative UI, not evidence
  of a real account.
- **Approved composition:** `.impeccable/mocks/focusflow-landing-comp-c.png`.
  The existing login-page navy/blue/violet/cyan visual language is binding.
  The composition approval is delegated by the user's instruction to rebuild
  the page like the login screen; the selected comp is the clearest expression
  of that stated direction.
- **Implementation inventory:** Brand and icons use Lucide; product preview,
  curved path, calendar, controls, and all text use semantic HTML/CSS/SVG;
  no raster asset ships in the UI.
- **Memorable moment:** The wide project-board, agenda, and calendar preview
  makes the destination of project work visible before the visitor acts.
